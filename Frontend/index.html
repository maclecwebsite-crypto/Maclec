Key Benefits

Every installation delivers measurable returns—financial, operational, and environmental—from day one.

Zero Civil Structure Needed

No dams, no reservoirs, no diversion channels. Install directly in existing waterways and start generating power within weeks.

24×7 Baseload Energy

Solar stops at sunset. Wind dies down. Flowing water never sleeps—delivering continuous, predictable power every hour of every day.

Zero Submergence & Displacement

No land is drowned. No communities are relocated. Fish and aquatic life move freely through the turbine zone untouched.

From Order to Operation in 3–12 Months

Factory-built modules ship ready to install. Minimal on-site civil work means you start earning from power generation within a single financial year.

Start Small, Scale to Gigawatts

Begin with a single 50 kW unit and expand incrementally. Add clusters as your energy demand grows—no upfront over-investment.

40+ Years of Reliable Generation

Built for decades of continuous operation in harsh water environments. Low wear parts mean minimal downtime and maintenance costs.

Canals, Rivers, Ports & Channels

One technology fits irrigation canals, hydropower tailraces, thermal cooling channels, tidal ports and pumped-storage systems.

100% Recyclable — 35–40% Value Back

Every component is fully recyclable. Even after the 40-year design life, you recover 35–40% of material value. True circular economy energy.

Lowest Cost of Energy (LCoE)

At just $0.03–$0.04 (3–4 cents) per kWh, SHKT delivers power cheaper than diesel, gas, and even grid parity solar-wind hybrids with storage.

Central Electricity Authority, Ministry of Power, Govt. of India

Committee Report on Hydro Kinetic Turbine Technology

A Committee headed by Member (Hydro), CEA — comprising representatives from CEA, MNRE, NHPC, THDCIL and IIT Roorkee — studied the concept and commercial applications of MACLEC's Surface Hydro Kinetic Turbine. Five meetings were held with presentations from MACLEC, GKinetic Energy (Ireland) and Imp Powers. The report covers technology concept, potential assessment, cost analysis, tariff framework and a roadmap for pilot installations.

  • Committee constituted under Member (Hydro), CEA
  • Five meetings with Indian & foreign technology presentations
  • TRL assessed at 6–7 (technology demonstration stage)
  • India potential estimated at GW scale
  • Indigenous cost benchmarked at ~₹8 Cr/MW
  • Levellised Tariff: ₹2.60–6.43 per unit
  • Recommended pilot projects before commercial scale-up

Academic & Technical Validation

Dr. R.P. Saini, MNRE Chair Professor (Dept. of Hydro & Renewable Energy), confirmed in a Sept 2020 letter that MACLEC is the industry partner in a UJVN-sponsored R&D project to develop the 100 kW VARUN-III surface hydrokinetic turbine. The prototype was tested on the Upper Ganga Canal, and the letter validates the novelty of MACLEC's partial-submergence approach for harnessing surface water velocity.

  • Formal R&D partnership with IIT Roorkee & UJVN Ltd.
  • VARUN-III prototype tested on Upper Ganga Canal
  • Novel partial-submergence design academically validated
  • Hydrodynamic performance & wake recovery assessed
