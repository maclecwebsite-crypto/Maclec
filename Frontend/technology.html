Turbine Configurations

Floating vs Fixed Turbines

Both configurations use the same core SHK Turbine technology — the right choice depends on the water body, width, depth and available head at your site.

Floating Turbines

Water Body

Rivers & canals with greater width and adequate depth

Design Capacity (Single Unit)

0.5 kW – 3000 kW

Best Suited For

Wide canals, rivers and reservoirs where the unit floats freely with flow and adapts to changing water levels

Fixed Turbines

Water Body

Small canals & rivers with less width and water depth

Design Capacity (Single Unit)

0.5 kW – 3000 kW

Best Suited For

Narrow channels, irrigation canals and shallow waterways where a fixed structural mount anchors the unit in place
